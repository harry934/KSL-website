Repair of Offshore Patrol Boats

Kenya Shipyards Limited (KSL) is a trusted partner in the repair and maintenance of Offshore Patrol Boats (OPBs), which serve as critical assets for maritime defense, law enforcement, and border protection operations. Our Mombasa and Kisumu shipyards are equipped with advanced facilities including slipways, workshops, and fabrication units capable of accommodating small and medium naval vessels. We cater to government security agencies such as the Kenya Navy, Coast Guard, and regional maritime authorities.

Our OPB repair services include hull and structural repairs, corrosion control, mechanical overhauls, and propeller shaft alignments. We also handle full engine diagnostics and servicing, including diesel engine tune-ups, fuel system calibration, gearbox and transmission repairs, and exhaust system replacements. Navigation and communication systems are inspected, upgraded, or replaced to ensure compliance with operational standards and modern maritime requirements.

We integrate modern diagnostic tools for fault detection, ensuring accuracy in damage assessment and timelines for return-to-service. All repairs are undertaken following IMO standards, class society requirements, and military specifications. Our team of naval architects and marine engineers work collaboratively to ensure structural integrity, fuel efficiency, and optimal performance.

KSL also provides preventive maintenance programs tailored to reduce downtime and extend vessel service life. By combining precision engineering, quality workmanship, and adherence to tight schedules, we help our clients keep their offshore patrol assets mission-ready and highly reliable in harsh marine environments

Repair and maintenance of tug boats

Kenya Shipyards Limited (KSL) offers specialized repair and maintenance services for tug boats—vital support vessels used for berthing, towing, and maneuvering larger ships in harbors, ports, and offshore locations. Our skilled technical teams and fully equipped shipyards in Mombasa and Kisumu are designed to handle both scheduled maintenance and emergency repairs, ensuring tugboats remain in optimal working condition for continuous operations.

Our comprehensive tug boat service covers routine inspections, diesel engine overhauls, gearbox repairs, hull cleaning and repainting, and propeller maintenance including polishing and balancing. We specialize in maintaining azimuth thrusters and conventional propulsion systems, ensuring powerful thrust and precise control during port operations. Hydraulic and pneumatic systems—often used in winches and towing mechanisms—are inspected and repaired to maintain full functionality and safety compliance.

Electrical systems, including navigation lights, communication radios, radar units, and onboard control panels, are thoroughly checked and restored to ensure seamless vessel operation and safety. Hull inspections, welding, and thickness gauging are carried out to prevent corrosion, increase durability, and maintain vessel certification.

We also provide dry docking services for major repairs and carry out ballast tank cleaning, repainting, and cathodic protection to minimize long-term wear. Our service is designed to meet the standards of classification societies such as Lloyd’s Register, DNV, and ABS.

KSL offers tailor-made maintenance schedules for port authorities, marine service providers, and industrial clients, helping to reduce operational downtimes and extend the service life of tug vessels, all while ensuring high safety and performance standards.

Rehabilitation and Remanufacture of Locomotives

At KSL, our engineering capabilities extend beyond marine vessels to include the repair and maintenance of locomotives supporting the critical connection between inland transport networks and maritime logistics. This service plays a vital role in enhancing port operations, especially for cargo movement between ports and inland depots.

Our locomotive workshop at KRC is equipped to handle diesel-electric and diesel-hydraulic locomotives commonly used by Kenya Railways Corporation in Kenya and Africa. Services include detailed mechanical inspections, complete engine overhauls, wheelset replacements, transmission system repairs, Rehabilitation and Remanufacturing. We also perform fuel injection tuning, cooling system upgrades, and turbocharger maintenance to enhance engine efficiency and performance.

Electrical system diagnostics and maintenance are key aspects of our locomotive services. Our experts repair or replace control modules, traction motors, wiring harnesses, alternators, and battery systems to restore electrical reliability and automation. We also calibrate braking systems and conduct air compressor repairs to ensure compliance with railway safety regulations.

KSL offers bogie inspections and alignment services, bearing replacements, suspension tuning, and frame straightening. These services reduce vibration, improve ride quality, and ensure smooth rail operations. Our team utilizes ultrasonic and magnetic particle testing to detect structural defects early, helping to prevent catastrophic failures.

Our preventive maintenance programs are designed to minimize breakdowns, extend asset life, and keep locomotives operational throughout the year. Whether supporting port railways or regional transport authorities, KSL delivers cost-effective and technically sound locomotive repair solutions tailored to enhance freight and passenger connectivity across the region.

Through continuous facility assessment, maintenance scheduling, and phased modernization, KSL ensures our shipyards remain resilient, efficient, and capable of supporting Kenya’s growing maritime ambitions and the broader Blue Economy strategy.

Repair and Maintenance of Research Vessels

Kenya Shipyards Limited (KSL) provides specialized repair and maintenance services for research vessels that serve universities, marine institutes, environmental agencies, and governmental bodies. These vessels are essential for conducting oceanographic, ecological, hydrographic, and fisheries research in Kenya’s waters and the wider East African coastline. Our shipyards are fully equipped to handle the complex systems and sensitive instrumentation onboard such vessels.

We offer end-to-end servicing for hull structures, propulsion systems, power generation units, and onboard laboratories. From repainting and anti-fouling to structural reinforcement, we ensure the vessel is seaworthy and compliant with international marine research standards. The vessels’ scientific instrumentation—such as sonar systems, echo sounders, water sampling gear, current meters, and seabed mapping tools—are calibrated, repaired, or replaced with the support of our electronics engineering team. We also maintain HVAC and refrigeration systems used to preserve biological samples and support onboard lab operations.

Electrical systems including data acquisition networks, battery banks, navigation suites, and satellite communication arrays are tested and upgraded for reliability in offshore missions. We ensure integration with modern technologies such as AIS, GPS, GIS, and real-time telemetry systems.

Additionally, KSL provides preventive maintenance packages that include periodic dry docking, safety inspections, life-saving appliance servicing, and class surveys to ensure uninterrupted field missions.

Our goal is to keep research vessels operational, accurate, and technologically equipped to support Kenya’s role in advancing marine science and protecting ocean resources through data-driven decisions.

Repair and Maintenance of Linkspans

Kenya Shipyards Limited (KSL) offers comprehensive repair and maintenance services for linkspans, which are critical components in port infrastructure enabling safe and efficient roll-on/roll-off (RoRo) operations. Used extensively in ferry terminals and cargo ports, linkspans create a moveable bridge between the shore and vessels, allowing vehicles and goods to be transferred seamlessly. Ensuring their structural integrity and functionality is vital to Kenya’s maritime logistics and commuter transport systems.

KSL provides both scheduled and emergency maintenance services for linkspans, including structural inspections, corrosion treatment, repainting, and load-bearing assessments. We specialize in refurbishing steel decks, access ramps, fenders, hinge points, and counterweight systems. Our engineers conduct non-destructive testing (NDT) to identify metal fatigue, cracks, or wear in critical load areas.

Hydraulic and mechanical systems that control linkspan movement are serviced to ensure smooth elevation and positioning during tidal shifts. We repair or replace hydraulic cylinders, pump units, hoses, valves, and control panels. Electrical components, such as control wiring, lighting, motorized drive systems, and sensors, are also tested and upgraded where necessary.

In addition, we ensure compliance with International Maritime Organization (IMO) and port authority safety standards by servicing guardrails, anti-slip surfaces, emergency lighting, and fire suppression systems. Our teams can deploy rapidly to support urgent linkspan rehabilitation in high-traffic ports across Kenya’s coastline and inland water bodies like Lake Victoria.

By delivering durable, safety-focused, and technically sound linkspan repair services, KSL helps ensure continuity in ferry operations, cargo transfers, and marine logistics throughout the region.

Repair and Maintenance of Cargo Vessels

Kenya Shipyards Limited (KSL) provides full-spectrum repair and maintenance services for cargo vessels operating along the Kenyan coast, inland lakes, and regional trade corridors. Our facilities in Mombasa and Kisumu are strategically positioned and equipped with dry docks, slipways, and heavy-duty workshops that can handle general cargo ships, bulk carriers, container vessels, and landing crafts. These services ensure that maritime logistics continue uninterrupted and efficiently across East Africa.

We carry out comprehensive inspections and repairs on hull structures, superstructures, and load-bearing decks. Our hull services include steel plate replacements, blasting, anti-fouling treatments, and structural reinforcements to preserve vessel strength and water-tight integrity. We perform ultrasonic thickness gauging and non-destructive testing (NDT) to assess corrosion and fatigue on steel frames and cargo holds.

Propulsion systems—including main engines, gearboxes, shafts, and propellers—are overhauled or replaced depending on operational wear. We service auxiliary engines, cooling systems, bilge pumps, and oil-water separators to maintain environmental compliance and machinery reliability. Electrical systems such as cargo lighting, load sensors, and power distribution networks are repaired and tested to support onboard operations and safety.

Our team also specializes in servicing cargo handling systems such as winches, cranes, hatches, and hydraulic ramps, ensuring efficient cargo loading and unloading. Life-saving appliances (LSA), fire-fighting equipment (FFE), and navigation systems are routinely inspected to meet class and port-state requirements.

By offering quick turnaround times, expert workmanship, and customized service contracts, KSL keeps cargo vessels operational, safe, and aligned with international maritime standards.

Dredging of Waterways

Kenya Shipyards Limited (KSL) provides specialized dredging services to ensure the navigability, safety, and functionality of Kenya’s ports, inland harbors, and waterways. Dredging plays a critical role in supporting maritime trade, preventing siltation, and maintaining sufficient water depths for vessel access—particularly in high-traffic areas like the Port of Mombasa, Lake Victoria, and industrial jetties.

Our dredging operations cover both capital dredging (for creating new channels or basins) and maintenance dredging (to restore existing water depths). We utilize a combination of mechanical and hydraulic dredging techniques, including cutter suction dredgers, backhoe dredgers, and grab dredgers, depending on the specific project requirements. Our technical team conducts pre-dredging hydrographic surveys to assess sediment buildup and channel contours using sonar and GPS mapping systems.

The dredged material is carefully evaluated for environmental impact and disposed of in accordance with NEMA and IMO guidelines, ensuring ecological balance and sustainability. In areas where sediment can be reused for land reclamation or construction fill, we offer reclamation services as an added value.

KSL also provides emergency dredging support in cases where waterways are blocked by sedimentation, floating debris, or storm damage, thereby restoring port access and minimizing vessel delays. Our services are critical for ferry terminals, cargo berths, naval bases, fishing harbors, and inland port facilities.

With a commitment to maintaining open and efficient waterways, KSL’s dredging solutions help boost maritime logistics, enhance regional connectivity, and support the long-term growth of the Blue Economy.

Repair and maintenance of passenger vessels

Kenya Shipyards Limited (KSL) provides specialized repair and maintenance services for passenger vessels, including ferries, water buses, cruise boats, and commuter crafts that operate across Kenya’s inland waterways and coastal routes. These vessels play a vital role in public transportation, tourism, and inter-island connectivity. KSL ensures they remain safe, efficient, and comfortable for both short and long-distance marine travel.

Our services include structural inspection and repairs to the hull, deck, and superstructure using certified marine-grade materials. We perform repainting, anti-fouling, and corrosion treatment to ensure long-term durability, especially in saline or brackish water environments. Passenger seating areas, cabins, washrooms, and walkways are refurbished or modified to meet ergonomic and hygiene standards.

The propulsion system—including main engines, gearboxes, propeller shafts, and steering systems—is inspected, repaired, or overhauled to ensure smooth navigation and reliability. We also service auxiliary power systems, bilge pumps, fresh water systems, and air conditioning units to support passenger comfort.

Our engineers check and repair electronic navigation equipment, including GPS, radar, AIS, and communication radios, ensuring compliance with maritime regulations and enhancing voyage safety. Life-saving appliances such as life rafts, life jackets, fire-fighting equipment, emergency alarms, and evacuation signage are tested and certified before vessel re-entry into service.

KSL supports both private operators and government-owned ferry services with tailored maintenance schedules, emergency breakdown repairs, and full dry-docking capabilities. Our goal is to ensure that passenger vessels under our care are seaworthy, safe, clean, and ready to deliver reliable transportation across Kenya’s maritime network.

Dry Docking Services

Kenya Shipyards Limited (KSL) offers professional dry docking services to support comprehensive inspection, maintenance, and repair of marine vessels. Our dry docks, located at Kisumu Shipyard and supported by slipway facilities in Mombasa, are designed to handle a wide range of vessels, including naval craft, cargo ships, ferries, tugs, barges, and passenger boats operating on Lake Victoria and along the Indian Ocean coastline.

Dry docking is a critical component of vessel lifecycle management, allowing for thorough below-the-waterline inspections and essential maintenance activities. At KSL, vessels are safely docked and supported on keel blocks, enabling our marine engineers and technicians to conduct extensive work on the hull, propeller shafts, rudders, sea chests, and underwater fittings.

Our dry docking services include:

  1. Hull cleaning, blasting, and repainting (including anti-fouling applications)
  2. Steel replacement and corrosion control
  3. Propulsion system alignment and repairs
  4. Sea valve and intake servicing
  5. Structural modifications and welding
  6. Inspection and servicing of rudders, stabilizers, and anode
  7. Dry dock testing and marine surveys for classification compliance

We adhere to strict safety, environmental, and regulatory standards throughout the dry docking process, and we support class inspections by DNV, Lloyd’s Register, Bureau Veritas, and other major bodies.

KSL provides scheduled dry docking packages, emergency docking solutions, and project-based repairs—all delivered on time and within budget. With skilled personnel, modern infrastructure, and a commitment to operational excellence, our dry docking services ensure vessel safety, longevity, and performance.

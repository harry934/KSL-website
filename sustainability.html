Sustainability

Building industry that lasts

At Kenya Shipyards Limited we build more than vessels. We build resilient industry, protect coastal and inland environments, and strengthen partnerships that lift communities and economies.

Guided by the UN Sustainable Development Goals, KSL integrates environmental, social, and governance principles across construction, repair, community engagement, and innovation.

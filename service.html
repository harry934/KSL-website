Maintenance, Repair and Overhaul

Repair of Offshore Patrol Boats

Hull, corrosion control, mechanical overhauls, shaft alignment, diesel diagnostics, and navigation systems for Navy, Coast Guard, and regional authorities. Work follows IMO, class, and military specifications.

Repair and maintenance of tug boats

Inspections, diesel overhauls, propellers, azimuth and conventional propulsion, winch hydraulics, and dry dock. Standards referenced include Lloyd's Register, DNV, and ABS.

Repair and Maintenance of Research Vessels

Hull, propulsion, laboratories, sonar, sampling gear, and data networks for universities, institutes, and agencies.

Repair and Maintenance of Cargo Vessels

General cargo, bulk, container vessels, and landing craft, including plate replacement, auxiliaries, cranes, and safety equipment.

Repair and maintenance of passenger vessels

Ferries, water buses, and commuter craft: hull, cabins, propulsion, and auxiliary power.

Dry Docking Services

Kisumu dry dock and Mombasa slipway. Hull treatment, shafts, rudders, sea chests, and class surveys.

Civil and Modular Works

Upgrade and Maintenance of Shipyards

Modernise dry docks, slipways, workshops, and quays, including CNC, welding, and environmental systems aligned to NEMA and IMO.

Repair and Maintenance of Linkspans

RoRo bridges: structure, hydraulics, electrics, anti slip surfaces, and fire systems for coastal and Lake Victoria ports.

Dredging of Waterways

Capital and maintenance dredging with cutter suction, backhoe, and grab methods. Disposal follows NEMA and IMO guidance.

Rehabilitation of Workshops

Turnkey rebuilds of structure, equipment, fire safety, lean layouts, and ICT for naval and regional dockyards.

Heavy Engineering

Rehabilitation and Remanufacture of Locomotives

Diesel electric and diesel hydraulic locomotives at the KRC workshop: engines, wheelsets, transmissions, traction motors, brakes, and bogies.

Leasing

Leasing Services

Vessels and plant, including cranes, telehandlers, boom lifts, forklifts, and scissor lifts, with optional operators and maintenance.

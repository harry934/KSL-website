About Kenya Shipyards Limited

Kenya Shipyards Limited was incorporated on 29 September 2020 to lead shipbuilding in Eastern Africa. It builds, repairs, and refits vessels for the Kenya Navy, ministries, departments and agencies, and other public and private clients.

KSL is a state corporation under the Ministry of Defence. Headquarters is at Herufi House, Treasury Buildings, Nairobi. The yards are in Mombasa, next to Kenya Navy Base at Mtongwe, and in Kisumu, next to the port along Marine Drive.

The company is ISO 9001 certified. Work covers new construction, maintenance and overhaul, civil and modular yard works, and heavy engineering.

What we do

Shipbuilding and engineering

Ship Construction

Patrol craft, ferries, and support vessels, designed and built for the Kenya Navy, MDAs, and regional partners.

Civil and Modular Works

Marine civil works, piers, jetties, linkspans, and modular structures that keep ports and yards operating.

Heavy Engineering

Large scale fabrication, locomotives, steel structures, and industrial plant for rail, energy, and defence.

Maintenance Repair and Overhaul

Scheduled refits, emergency repairs, and dry docking to keep fleets mission ready and class compliant.

GRP Boats

Light, durable GRP hulls for patrol, fisheries, ambulance response, and county water transport.

Why Choose

Kenya Shipyards Limited?

With 5 years of experience in the shipbuilding industry, we don’t just build ships we build trust. With ISO 9001 certification and strategically located facilities in Mombasa and Kisumu, we offer world class infrastructure backed by a skilled, certified and readily available workforce. Total quality, safety, and cost efficiency are at the core of every project.

As a state corporation under the Ministry of Defence we operate with discipline, accountability and high standards.

Choosing KSL means choosing experience, commitment, and a trusted partner.

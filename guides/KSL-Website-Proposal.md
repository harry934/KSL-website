# Proposal: Kenya Shipyards Limited Corporate Website

**Prepared for:** Kenya Shipyards Limited (KSL)
**Date:** October 2026
**Document type:** Brief system proposal and functional overview

---

## 1. Summary

This proposal presents the new corporate website for Kenya Shipyards Limited. The website is the public face of KSL. It explains who the corporation is, what it builds and repairs, and how the public, clients, partners and job seekers can reach it.

The site is fast, works well on phones, and is open to everyone. It includes accessibility tools for people with visual or motor difficulties, and it can be read in English, Kiswahili and many foreign languages.

## 2. Objectives

1. Present KSL's mandate, leadership and capabilities clearly and professionally.
2. Make services, products and projects easy to find and understand.
3. Give the public quick access to official documents, tenders, news and events.
4. Make it simple to contact KSL through phone, email, WhatsApp or the contact form.
5. Serve every visitor, whatever their device, language or ability.

## 3. Scope: Pages in the System

| Area | Pages |
|---|---|
| Corporate | Home, About KSL, KSL Board, Organization Structure |
| What we do | Services, Products, Projects, Sustainability, CSR |
| Media | News and Events, News article, Event page, Gallery |
| Public information | Downloads (corporate documents), FAQ, Careers |
| Engagement | Contact Us, plus a "page not found" page that guides lost visitors back |

## 4. Main Features and What They Do

### 4.1 Home page
- A full-width opening banner with KSL's message, *Catalyzing Shipbuilding Industry*.
- KSL's working values (Quality, Improvement, Customers, Partnerships, Pricing) presented as quick cards.
- Highlights of services, key figures (projects delivered, years of operation, shipyards), clients, partners and the latest news.
- An interactive map of the Mombasa and Kisumu shipyards with a satellite view of each location.

### 4.2 About KSL, Board and Organization
- KSL's story, incorporation dates, vision, mission and mandate.
- A milestone timeline from the first yard works to the launch of MV Uhuru II.
- Core values, strategic pillars and the specifications of both shipyards.
- Board of Directors profiles. These can be filtered by group (Defence, Principal Secretaries, Independent members) and opened for full details.
- The organization structure, from the Board down to each department.

### 4.3 Services, Products and Projects
- Services are grouped into tabs: Maintenance, Repair and Overhaul; Civil and Modular Works; Heavy Engineering; and Leasing.
- Each service opens a detail window with a photo, a full description and related documents to view.
- Product and project pages show KSL's vessels and completed work.

### 4.4 News, Events and Gallery
- A news and events feed with search, so visitors can find stories by keyword.
- Full article and event pages. Event pages show the date, time and venue, with directions.
- Share buttons for Facebook, X, LinkedIn and WhatsApp, plus a "copy link" button.
- A photo gallery that opens images in a full-screen viewer with swipe support on phones.

### 4.5 Downloads (Corporate Documents)
- One library for official documents: strategic plan, service charter, tender documents, brochures, complaint forms and access-to-information forms.
- Search and filter by category, with a clear "View document" button on every file.

### 4.6 Careers and FAQ
- Careers lists open vacancies with a filter by location. When no roles are open, it explains how to send a CV.
- FAQ answers common questions, with a search box that narrows the answers as the visitor types.

### 4.7 Contact and Communication
- A contact form that checks for missing details before sending the message to KSL's public affairs email.
- Phone numbers, email addresses and office locations (Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu) on every page.
- A WhatsApp button for quick chat with KSL.
- A site-wide search available from the header and mobile menu.

## 5. Inclusivity and Accessibility

The website is built to be usable by everyone:

- **Accessibility panel:** visitors can enlarge text, switch to a more readable font, underline or highlight links, and turn on keyboard navigation. They can also make images grey, invert colours, use a dark "lights off" mode, remove animations or remove styling. These settings help people with low vision, colour blindness, dyslexia or motion sensitivity.
- **Languages:** a language menu offers English and Kiswahili, which are written by hand. It also offers more than 240 African and world languages through automatic translation, and each language is shown in its own script (for example, العربية, 中文, हिन्दी).
- **Screen readers and keyboards:** pages use proper headings, labels and descriptions, so assistive technology can read them correctly.

## 6. Mobile Experience

- The layout adapts to any screen, from phones to large monitors.
- On phones, a bottom navigation bar gives one-tap access to Home, Services, Contact and the full menu.
- Content stays steady during fast scrolling, spacing is consistent, and text is kept at a readable size.
- Pop-up windows are sized for small screens and hide other floating buttons while open.

## 7. Content Management

News, events, services, downloads, careers, FAQ, gallery and the organization structure are stored in simple data files, separate from the page design. Staff can add a news story, post a vacancy or publish a document by updating one file. No changes to the page layout are needed.

## 8. Technical Overview

- A lightweight website built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript on the Bootstrap framework.
- No database or server software is required, so it is fast, low-cost to host and has a small security risk surface.
- Images load only when needed, and files are versioned so visitors always receive the latest updates.
- Source code is kept under version control on GitHub, so every change is tracked and can be reversed.

## 9. Benefits to KSL

- A modern, trustworthy image that matches KSL's role as a state corporation.
- Better public access to information, supporting transparency and the Access to Information Act.
- Fewer routine enquiries, because answers, documents and contacts are easy to find.
- Wider reach to local and international clients through mobile access and multiple languages.
- Low running costs and easy updates by KSL staff.

## 10. Recommended Next Steps

1. Review and approve the content on each page with the relevant departments.
2. Confirm the hosting provider and the official domain.
3. Connect the contact form to a dedicated mail service so messages arrive directly, instead of through the visitor's own email app.
4. Add visitor analytics to measure which pages and documents are used most.
5. Train communications staff to update news, events, vacancies and documents.

1Executive Summary

This proposal presents the redesigned corporate website of Kenya Shipyards Limited. The new website builds on the corporation's existing online presence and improves it in design, content, accessibility and ease of use. It explains who KSL is, what it builds and repairs, and how the public, clients, partners and job seekers can reach the corporation.

The redesigned site is fast, works well on phones and is open to everyone. It includes accessibility tools for people with visual or motor difficulties, and it can be read in English, Kiswahili and many other languages.

2Background

KSL's previous website served as the corporation's first point of contact online, giving the public basic information about its mandate, services and contacts. Since then, KSL's operations have grown across the Mombasa and Kisumu shipyards, the number of official publications, tenders and news updates has increased, and more visitors now reach the site from mobile phones.

The redesign responds to this growth. Rather than replacing KSL's online identity, it retains the corporation's name, branding, colours and core information, and presents them in a modern, well-organised and more accessible form that reflects KSL's standing as a state corporation.

3Objectives of the Redesign

  1. Present KSL's mandate, leadership and capabilities clearly and professionally.
  2. Make services, products and projects easier to find and understand.
  3. Give the public quick access to official documents, tenders, news and events.
  4. Make it simple to contact KSL by phone, email, WhatsApp or the contact form.
  5. Serve every visitor, whatever their device, language or ability.

6Main Features and What They Do

6.1 Home page

  • A full-width opening banner carrying KSL's message, Catalyzing Shipbuilding Industry.
  • KSL's working values (Quality, Improvement, Customers, Partnerships and Pricing) presented as concise cards.
  • Highlights of services, key figures (projects delivered, years of operation and shipyards), clients, partners and the latest news.
  • An interactive map of the Mombasa and Kisumu shipyards with a satellite view of each location.

6.2 About KSL, Board and Organization

  • KSL's story, incorporation dates, vision, mission and mandate.
  • A milestone timeline from the first yard works to the launch of MV Uhuru II.
  • Core values, strategic pillars and the specifications of both shipyards.
  • Board of Directors profiles, which can be filtered by group (Defence, Principal Secretaries and Independent members) and opened for full details.
  • The organization structure, from the Board down to each department.

6.3 Services, Products and Projects

  • Services grouped into tabs: Maintenance, Repair and Overhaul; Civil and Modular Works; Heavy Engineering; and Leasing.
  • Each service opens a detail window with a photograph, a full description and related documents.
  • Product and project pages that showcase KSL's vessels and completed work.

6.4 News, Events and Gallery

  • A news and events feed with search, so visitors can find stories by keyword.
  • Full article and event pages; event pages show the date, time and venue, with directions.
  • Share buttons for Facebook, X, LinkedIn and WhatsApp, together with a "copy link" option.
  • A photo gallery that opens images in a full-screen viewer, with swipe support on phones.

6.5 Downloads (Corporate Documents)

  • One library for official documents: the strategic plan, service charter, tender documents, brochures, complaint forms and access-to-information forms.
  • Search and category filters, with a clear "View document" button on every file.

6.6 Careers and FAQ

  • Careers lists open vacancies with a filter by location. When no roles are open, it explains how to submit a CV.
  • The FAQ answers common questions, with a search box that narrows the answers as the visitor types.

6.7 Contact and Communication

  • A contact form that checks for missing details before sending the message to KSL's public affairs office.
  • Phone numbers, email addresses and office locations (Nairobi, Mombasa and Kisumu) on every page.
  • A WhatsApp button for quick conversations with KSL.
  • A site-wide search available from the header and the mobile menu.

7Inclusivity and Accessibility

The redesigned website is built to be usable by everyone:

  • Accessibility panel: visitors can enlarge text, switch to a more readable font, underline or highlight links and turn on keyboard navigation. They can also make images grey, invert colours, use a dark "lights off" mode, stop animations or remove styling. These settings assist people with low vision, colour blindness, dyslexia or motion sensitivity.
  • Languages: a language menu offers English and Kiswahili, both written by hand, as well as more than 240 African and world languages through automatic translation. Each language is shown in its own script (for example, العربية, 中文 and हिन्दी).
  • Screen readers and keyboards: pages use proper headings, labels and descriptions, so that assistive technology reads them correctly.

8Mobile Experience

  • The layout adapts to any screen, from phones to large monitors.
  • On phones, a bottom navigation bar gives one-tap access to Home, Services, Contact and the full menu.
  • Content remains steady during fast scrolling, spacing is consistent and text is kept at a readable size.
  • Pop-up windows are sized for small screens and hide other floating buttons while open.

9Content Management

News, events, services, downloads, careers, FAQ, the gallery and the organization structure are stored in simple data files, separate from the page design. Staff can add a news story, post a vacancy or publish a document by updating a single file, without any change to the page layout.

10Technical Overview

  • A lightweight website built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript on the Bootstrap framework.
  • No database or server software is required, which keeps the site fast, inexpensive to host and limits its exposure to security risks.
  • Images load only when needed, and files are versioned so that visitors always receive the latest updates.
  • The source code is kept under version control, so every change is recorded and can be reversed.

11Benefits to KSL

  • A modern, trustworthy image that matches KSL's role as a state corporation.
  • Better public access to information, in support of transparency and the Access to Information Act.
  • Fewer routine enquiries, because answers, documents and contacts are easy to find.
  • Wider reach to local and international clients through mobile access and multiple languages.
  • Low running costs and straightforward updates by KSL staff.

12Conclusion

The redesigned website preserves the identity and information that KSL's audiences already know, while presenting them in a clearer, more accessible and more professional manner. It equips the corporation with a reliable digital platform that can grow alongside its shipbuilding, repair and engineering work.
